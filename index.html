Human–AI · XR2026 — Present

Situated human-agent reasoning in AR

How can a wearable agent expose a task understanding that is grounded in the scene and can actually be revised when the user provides new information?

I developed a wearer-facing reasoning model that connects task-relevant observations to spatial references, task state, and later revisions. The runtime combines egocentric visual interpretation with detector-backed localization and Quest geometry, while human corrections update only the conclusions that depend on them rather than restarting the task.

My role: research framing, system architecture, reasoning/state representation, perception-grounding pipeline, interaction design, and experimental instrumentation.

Purdue C Design Lab · advised by Prof. Karthik Ramani
XR Authoring · Agents2025 — 2026

In-situ authoring for embodied agents in AR

How should creators specify agent behavior when the meaning of an interaction depends on where a person is looking, pointing, standing, and moving?

I designed a dual-view authoring workflow that lets creators define interactions in the physical scene while editing task structure in a world-in-miniature. A structured task representation keeps spatial context and task flow explicit, and a multimodal compiler turns speech, gaze, pointing, and pose into executable agent behaviors.

My role: interaction concept, task representation, multimodal compiler/runtime, Unity/Meta Quest implementation, and evaluation. A two-session study with 12 participants achieved a 79.8 ± 10.8 SUS score.

Purdue C Design Lab · advised by Prof. Karthik Ramani
Egocentric AI · Memory2026 — Present

Long-horizon memory for wearable AI

How can an assistant recover the right moment, object state, or interaction from hours of first-person experience without repeatedly reprocessing the full stream?

I work on event-centric representations, hierarchical retrieval, and tool-based inspection for grounded event recall, temporal localization, and multimodal question answering over hour- to day-scale egocentric video. The broader goal is memory that preserves useful world changes instead of becoming an ever-growing transcript.

My role: long-video reasoning, retrieval design, data curation, post-training support, benchmark design, and accuracy–latency evaluation for intelligent eyewear.

Egocentric AI research · supervised by Dr. Jingkang Yang
3D Generation · Evaluation2025 — Present

Contact-aware 3D piano hand motion generation

Can generated motion be optimized for whether it actually plays the right notes at the right time, rather than only for pose similarity?

I reframed MIDI-conditioned bimanual motion around time-indexed 3D key-contact targets and developed contact-aware flow-matching objectives. I also designed a hard-passage evaluation protocol for large leaps, dense chords, and rapid transitions so generation quality is judged against the physical task.

My role: problem formulation, target representation, learning objectives, event-local refinement, and task-specific evaluation.

Purdue ECE · advised by Prof. Yung-Hsiang Lu
Generative AI · Edge AI2025 — Present

Hardware-aware evaluation of text-to-image models

When is a stronger generative model actually worth its latency and memory cost on an edge device?

I built a benchmark for seven text-to-image models that evaluates semantic alignment, perceptual quality, diversity, end-to-end latency, and peak GPU memory together. All models were deployed on NVIDIA Jetson Orin to make capability–efficiency trade-offs directly comparable.

My role: benchmark design, prompt taxonomy, automated evaluators, edge deployment, and systems profiling.

Purdue ECE · advised by Prof. Yung-Hsiang Lu
Wearable AI · Voice AgentsSummer 2026

Voice-first video assistance for AR glasses

How can a hands-free assistant resolve vague video requests without forcing the user through a screen-heavy search workflow?

At Eyedaptic, I built a voice-first agent that manages search, clarification, selection, history resume, and playback through typed tools. I also designed a transparent two-stage retrieval and reranking pipeline; on a 10-scenario evaluation, mean top-1 score improved from 0.182 to 0.604.

My role: agent/tool architecture, retrieval and ranking, local behavioral memory, Android integration, and evaluation.

Prototype work: I also build smart-glasses agents that connect multimodal requests to visual grounding, cross-frame retrieval, and verified tool execution. These prototypes are where I test ideas quickly before deciding which questions deserve a full study.

About
