Research

I am interested in what changes when AI leaves the chatbox and has to share a world with people.

For a physical-world assistant, strong perception alone is not enough. It has to connect what it sees now with what happened before, reason over incomplete evidence, and stay aligned with what the person is trying to accomplish. I use working systems to study that full loop.

Spatial grounding, multimodal sensing, scene understanding, and task-relevant perception.

02

CoReason

How can a wearable agent make its understanding of a physical task visible, grounded, and revisable instead of returning opaque guidance?

I designed a Quest-based co-reasoning system that exposes task-relevant observations and their implications while tying them to objects in the scene. The runtime combines egocentric VLM interpretation, open-vocabulary localization, Quest depth/pose, and persistent task state; new visual or human evidence updates only the reasoning it affects.

My role: research framing, interaction model, runtime architecture, perception/grounding pipeline, revision logic, Unity/Quest implementation, and study instrumentation. Advised by Prof. Karthik Ramani ↗.

2025–2026Under ReviewC Design Lab ↗

SituatAR

How can creators author embodied agent behavior when interaction depends on where people look, point, move, and act in space?

I designed a dual-view AR authoring workflow that pairs in-situ specification with world-in-miniature editing and represents experiences as explicit task and spatial structure. I also built the LLM-backed compiler/runtime that grounds speech, gaze, pointing, and user pose to scene referents and converts them into executable agent behaviors.

My role: authoring workflow, interaction representation, multimodal grounding/compiler stack, Unity/Meta Quest runtime, and system evaluation. In a 12-participant study, SituatAR achieved a 79.8 ± 10.8 SUS score.

Long-Horizon Egocentric Memory

How should a wearable assistant turn hours of first-person experience into memory it can search and reason over efficiently?

I am developing event-centric memory and hierarchical retrieval for long-form egocentric video, with tool-based inspection for event recall, temporal localization, and multimodal question answering. The goal is to recover the right evidence without repeatedly processing an entire hour- or day-scale stream.

My role: event representation, retrieval/reasoning pipeline, data and evaluation work, and deployment-oriented latency analysis. Supervised by Dr. Jingkang Yang ↗.

Accuracy-Oriented 3D Piano Hand Motion

Can generated hand motion be judged by whether it actually plays the right notes, rather than only by pose similarity?

I reframed MIDI-to-hand motion around time-indexed 3D key-contact targets and developed contact-aware flow-matching objectives for bimanual generation. I also designed a difficulty-aware evaluation protocol for passages where wrong fingers, timing errors, and large hand transitions matter most.

My role: problem formulation, contact constraints, flow-matching objectives, event-local refinement, and task-specific evaluation. Advised by Prof. Yung-Hsiang Lu ↗.

Hardware-Aware Text-to-Image Evaluation

What quality does a generative model actually buy for the latency and memory it costs on edge hardware?

I built a benchmark spanning seven text-to-image models, compositional prompt categories, task-specific semantic evaluators, and perceptual metrics, then deployed every model on NVIDIA Jetson Orin for end-to-end latency and peak-memory profiling.

My role: benchmark design, prompt taxonomy, evaluator integration, Jetson deployment, profiling, and quality–efficiency analysis.

Eva — Voice-First Video Assistant for AR Glasses

How can a hands-free assistant resolve vague video requests without forcing the user through a screen-heavy search flow?

I built a tool-using assistant that converts underspecified speech into searchable intent, handles clarification and selection, and controls playback and resume on AR glasses. I designed an interpretable retrieval/reranking pipeline over YouTube candidates, metadata, channel context, and local behavioral memory.

My role: agent/tool architecture, retrieval and ranking, personalization memory, Android/backend integration, and scenario evaluation. Mean top-1 score improved from 0.182 to 0.604 across a 10-scenario test set.

About

I like research questions that force model capability and interaction design to meet.

Most of my projects become working systems because prototypes expose failures that are hard to see in a benchmark alone: missing context, noisy sensing, latency, ambiguous user intent, or an explanation that is technically correct but unusable in the moment.

I am currently preparing PhD applications for Fall 2027 and am especially interested in research groups working across human-centered AI, spatial intelligence, egocentric/wearable computing, and XR.
