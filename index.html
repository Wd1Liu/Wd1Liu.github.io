Human–AI · XR2026 — PresentManuscript under review

Situated human–AI reasoning in AR

How can an AI assistant make its understanding of a physical task visible, grounded, and easy to revise?

I developed a spatially grounded co-reasoning prototype in which task-relevant observations, interpretations, and user contributions are represented as explicit, revisable task state rather than disappearing into a dialogue history. The system combines egocentric visual semantics with detector-backed references and Quest geometry, so public claims stay anchored to visible evidence and user corrections propagate only through the state that depends on them.

My role: research framing, reasoning and state representation, system architecture, perception-grounding pipeline, interaction design, Unity/Quest implementation, and study instrumentation.

Purdue C Design Lab ↗ · advised by Prof. Karthik Ramani ↗
XR Authoring · Agents2025 — 2026Manuscript under review

In-situ authoring for embodied agents in AR

How can creators author agent behavior without translating situated intent into low-level scripts?

I designed a dual-view authoring workflow that combines in-situ interaction specification with a world-in-miniature overview, using an explicit hierarchical task representation to preserve spatial context across authored behaviors. I also built the multimodal compiler/runtime that maps speech, gaze, pointing, and user pose to scene referents and executable agent behaviors.

My role: interaction concept, task representation, multimodal grounding/compiler stack, Unity/Meta Quest runtime, and evaluation. A two-session study with 12 participants achieved a 79.8 ± 10.8 SUS score.

Purdue C Design Lab ↗ · advised by Prof. Karthik Ramani ↗
Egocentric AI · Memory2026 — Present

Long-horizon memory for wearable AI

How can wearable AI compress hours or days of first-person experience into memory that remains useful for later reasoning?

I work on event-centric representations, hierarchical retrieval, and tool-based evidence inspection for grounded event recall, temporal localization, and multimodal question answering over long-form egocentric video. I am especially interested in memory that preserves entities, interactions, and state changes instead of becoming an ever-growing transcript.

My role: long-video reasoning, retrieval design, data curation, post-training support, benchmark design, and accuracy–latency analysis for intelligent eyewear.

Egocentric AI research · supervised by Dr. Jingkang Yang ↗
3D Generation · Evaluation2025 — Present

Contact-aware 3D piano hand motion generation

Can generated motion be judged by whether it actually plays the right notes, rather than only by pose similarity?

I reframed MIDI-conditioned bimanual motion around time-indexed 3D key-contact targets and developed contact-aware flow-matching objectives. I also designed a hard-passage evaluation protocol for large leaps, dense chords, and rapid transitions so generation quality is measured against the physical task itself.

My role: problem formulation, target representation, learning objectives, event-local refinement, and task-specific evaluation.

Purdue ECE ↗ · advised by Prof. Yung-Hsiang Lu ↗
Generative AI · Edge AI2025 — Present

Hardware-aware evaluation of text-to-image models

When is a stronger generative model actually worth its latency and memory cost on an edge device?

I built a benchmark for seven text-to-image models that evaluates semantic alignment, perceptual quality, diversity, end-to-end latency, and peak GPU memory together. Deploying each model on NVIDIA Jetson Orin made capability–efficiency trade-offs directly comparable rather than reporting image quality in isolation.

My role: benchmark design, prompt taxonomy, automated evaluators, edge deployment, and systems profiling.

Purdue ECE ↗ · advised by Prof. Yung-Hsiang Lu ↗
Wearable AI · Voice AgentsSummer 2026

Voice-first video assistance for AR glasses

How can a hands-free assistant resolve vague video requests without forcing the user through a screen-heavy search flow?

At Eyedaptic ↗, I built a tool-using assistant that turns underspecified speech into searchable intent, manages clarification and selection, and controls playback and resume on AR glasses. I also designed an interpretable two-stage retrieval/reranking pipeline over candidate videos, metadata, channel context, and local behavioral memory.

My role: agent/tool architecture, retrieval and ranking, personalization memory, Android/backend integration, and scenario evaluation. Mean top-1 score improved from 0.182 to 0.604 across a 10-scenario test set.

Prototype work. I also build smart-glasses agents that connect multimodal requests to visual grounding, cross-frame retrieval, and verified tool execution. I use these prototypes to test ideas quickly before deciding which questions deserve a full study.

About

I like problems that become clearer when you trace them back to how people actually think.

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Purdue University ↗. I work across models, systems, and interaction design because physical-world AI breaks quickly when those pieces are optimized in isolation.

I am also interested in entrepreneurship and creating new products. I often start from the user's mental model—what does the person believe this system is doing?—before deciding what technology or interface should exist around it.
